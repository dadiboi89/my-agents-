## 3. Scenario taxonomy (what episodes are "about")

Every episode = **one everyday toddler scenario** turned into a song:

1. **Discovery/nature** — beach animals, jungle animals, "what lives where?" *(top performers)*
2. **Games kids already play** — floor is lava, hide and seek, ball games *(imports playground trends)*
3. **Imagination play** — toys/sandcastles come alive, pretend journeys
4. **Family routines** — bedtime, meals, bath, getting dressed *(sleep = biggest parent pain point)*
5. **Celebrations** — puppy day, birthdays, holidays, seasons
6. **Gentle emotional beats** — small mishap (knocked-over sandcastle, scraped knee) → comfort → resolution *(teaches co-regulation; parents love it)*

Educational payload embedded in every scenario: animal names + sounds, counting, colors, body movement, simple social-emotional lessons.

## 4. Hook taxonomy

### Title hooks (formula: `[Hook] + emoji + | [Series] | [Channel + keyword suffix]`)
1. **Question hook** — *"Che animali vivono in spiaggia?"* → curiosity + matches parent search queries. **Best performer.**
2. **Invitation/imperative hook** — *"Giochiamo a…!", "Festeggiamo…!"* ("Let's play/celebrate") → inclusive "we".
3. **Trending-game hook** — *"Il Pavimento è Lava!"* → borrows existing kid-culture search volume.
4. **Parent-pain-point hook** — *"Come fare quando il tuo bimbo non vuole dormire!"* → literally a parenting-advice headline on a kids' compilation. Targets the *chooser*, not the viewer.
5. **Seasonal hook** — *"Estate con CoComelon!"* playlist branding.
- Every title ends with the SEO suffix **"CoComelon Italiano – Canzoni per Bambini"** → owns the search term *canzoni per bambini*.
- Exactly 1–2 emojis per title, matching the topic (🐵 ⚽ 🏖 🌋 🐕).

### Thumbnail hooks
- 1–2 **huge character faces**, direct eye contact, exaggerated emotion (the sleep compilation shows the *mom's* worried face — parent-relatable).
- Saturated primary colors, simple background matching the scenario.
- **"Italiano" corner badge** (localization flag), logo bottom-left, **"90 min" duration badge** on compilations (value signal to parents).

### Cold-open hooks (first 15–20 seconds, before any singing)
- Characters mid-scenario + a spoken **question to camera** ("What's that in the water?!") → toddler leans in, then the intro jingle fires.

### Retention mechanics inside the video
- One new "unit" (animal/action) every 30–45 s, same musical chorus between units (novelty + familiarity loop).
- **Call-and-response**: "Can YOU hop like the bunny?" → physical participation = no drop-off.
- Onomatopoeia everywhere (splash, clic-clac, whoosh) — pre-verbal kids stay hooked on sound.
- A **mid-video pattern interrupt** (~50% mark): small dramatic "oh no!" beat with music change, resolved warmly.
- Recap verse at the end (name everything you met = learning payoff + natural conclusion).

---

## 5. Replicable channel formula (checklist)

1. ~5–6 min single-scenario song videos as the discovery engine (2–4/month).
2. Re-bundle every 8–12 songs into 60–90 min compilations with **parent-pain-point or celebration titles** (watch-time engine).
3. Seasonal playlists rotated on the channel home.
4. Question-hook titles on discovery videos; advice-hook titles on compilations.
5. Consistent brand block: mascot logo, jingle, title suffix, corner language badge.
6. Localize a proven global format into an underserved language market (that's literally what CoComelon Italiano is — a localization).

# 6. ORIGINAL SCRIPT — same format, new IP

## "Chi Vive nel Mare? 🐠 | Canzoni per Bambini"
*(English: "Who Lives in the Sea?")*

**Original brand:** **Limoncino TV** — mascot: a smiling little lemon with sunglasses. Characters: **Nino** (baby, ~2 yrs, lemon-yellow curl), big sister **Gaia** (5 yrs), **Mamma**, dog **Biscotto**.
**Why this topic:** clones the traits of the channel's two biggest hits (beach + sea animals + question hook + imagination frame) and lands inside the summer window.

- **Runtime:** ~5:30 · **Style:** bright 3D toddler animation · **Tempo:** 96 BPM, ukulele + glockenspiel + soft percussion
- **Title (A):** `Chi Vive nel Mare? 🐠 | Limoncino TV – Canzoni per Bambini`
- **Title (B, test):** `Che animali si nascondono nel mare? 🐙 | Limoncino TV – Canzoni per Bambini`
- **Thumbnail:** Nino + Gaia's faces huge in foreground pointing at a smiling octopus popping out of blue water; "Italiano" badge top-left, Limoncino logo bottom-left.

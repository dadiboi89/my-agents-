## ANTI-AI AESTHETIC CHECKLIST (apply to every generation)

- [ ] No perfect symmetry — slight head tilt, off-center framing
- [ ] Motion starts mid-action, not from a still pose
- [ ] Breathing/micro-movements visible
- [ ] No studio lighting — window, gym fluorescent, bedroom lamp
- [ ] Hair has texture, flyaways okay
- [ ] Expression reaches eyes, not just mouth
- [ ] Camera has slight drift or handheld feel
- [ ] Background is real (gym, home, outside) — never plain backdrop
